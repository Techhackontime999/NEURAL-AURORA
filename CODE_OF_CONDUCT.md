# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful and considerate of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Assuming good intent and focusing on solutions
- Showing empathy and kindness toward others
- Helping others learn and grow

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, trolling, or derogatory comments
- Discriminatory language or exclusionary behavior
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Spam, self-promotion unrelated to the project, or disruptive behavior
- Any conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable behavior. They have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within project spaces (GitHub issues, PRs, discussions, Discord/other official channels) and when representing the project publicly.

## Reporting

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by emailing **techhackontime999@gmail.com** with subject `CODE OF CONDUCT REPORT`.

All reports will be reviewed, investigated, and handled with discretion.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Maintainers will follow these guidelines in determining consequences:

1. **Correction** – Private warning, clarification of behavior
2. **Warning** – Formal warning with consequences for continued behavior
3. **Temporary Ban** – Temporary removal from community spaces
4. **Permanent Ban** – Permanent removal for repeated violations or egregious behavior
