# PRIVACY POLICY

**Last Updated:** October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **NEURAL AURORA** ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our website/software and digital store.

## 1. Information We Collect

### Information you provide
- **Checkout details** – Email address and optional phone number (collected at purchase). Phone numbers are normalized to digits-only international form and used only to help deliver sign-in links (WhatsApp shortcut). They are never used for authentication.
- **Contact/Reviews** – Name, email, message, rating (if submitted via forms)
- **Authentication** – Email address when signing in (via Supabase Auth)

### Automatically collected
- **Technical data** – IP address, browser/user agent, device info, request logs (standard server/function logs)
- **Usage data** – Page views, feature usage (analytics-lite as implemented)

### Payment data
- Payments are processed by **Razorpay**. We do **not** store raw card/UPI details. Razorpay handles payment data under their [Privacy Policy](https://razorpay.com/privacy/).

## 2. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:
- Process orders and deliver purchased digital products
- Verify access to entitlements and prevent abuse
- Provide authentication and account access
- Respond to inquiries, messages, and support requests
- Improve stability, security, and user experience
- Maintain audit logs for security and fraud prevention

## 3. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do **not** sell or rent your personal information. We may share it only with:
- **Service providers** – [Supabase](https://supabase.com/privacy) (auth, database, storage), [Razorpay](https://razorpay.com/privacy/) (payments), and hosting providers (Vercel) as necessary to operate the service
- **Legal compliance** – When required by law, valid legal request, or to protect rights/safety
- **With your consent** – When you explicitly authorize sharing

## 4. Data Storage & Security

- Data is stored in Supabase (PostgreSQL). Row-Level Security (RLS) enforces least-privilege access.
- Paid files are stored in a **private** Supabase Storage bucket; access is only via short-lived signed URLs after entitlement verification.
- Secrets (API keys, webhook secrets, service-role keys) are stored in secure environment variables, never in the repository or client code.
- We apply reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect your data. No method is 100% secure.

## 5. Data Retention

We retain order, entitlement, and transaction audit data for as long as reasonably necessary for accounting, fraud prevention, support, and legal compliance. You may request deletion of your personal data (see Section 6), subject to legal/financial retention obligations.
