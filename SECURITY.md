# Security Policy

## Reporting a Vulnerability

If you discover a security vulnerability in **NEURAL AURORA**, please **do not** open a public GitHub issue.

Instead, report it privately to:

**Email:** amitkumarkh01012006@gmail.com  
**Subject:** `SECURITY: NEURAL AURORA`

## What to Include

Please include as much detail as possible:

- A clear description of the vulnerability
- Steps to reproduce (proof of concept if available)
- Potential impact (confidentiality/integrity/availability)
- Affected versions/commit hash if known
- Suggested fix (optional but appreciated)

## Response Timeline

- **Acknowledgement** – Within 48 hours of receiving your report
- **Initial Assessment** – Within 3–5 business days
- **Fix & Disclosure** – We aim to resolve critical issues as quickly as possible. Coordinated disclosure is preferred.

## Scope

This policy covers:
- The web app (`src/`, `api/`)
- Serverless functions (`api/*`)
- Supabase schema/RLS logic
- Authentication, authorization & digital store flows
- Build/deployment configuration

Out of scope:
- Third-party dependencies (report to their maintainers)
- Social engineering attacks
- Issues already known or fixed

## Responsible Disclosure

We appreciate your efforts to responsibly disclose vulnerabilities and will credit you (if desired) after a fix is released.

Thank you for helping keep NEURAL AURORA secure.
